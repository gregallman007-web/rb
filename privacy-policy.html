Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 October 2026

01Who we are

Rathbone Advertising Ltd ("Rathbone Advertising", "Rathbone", "R.A.", "we", "us" or "our") is a UK advertising agency providing advertising, media planning, media buying and related marketing services.

We act as the data controller for personal information we collect and process for our own business purposes, in accordance with the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR), the Data Protection Act 2018 and other applicable data protection legislation.

Our company details are:

  • Company name: Rathbone Advertising Ltd
  • Company registration number: 13205398
  • Registered office: 146 High Street, Billericay, England, CM12 9DF
  • Website: https://www.rathboneadvertising.com
  • Email: contact@rathboneadvertising.com

02Personal information we collect

We may collect and process the following types of personal information:

  • Enquiry information: your name, email address, company name, job title and any information you provide through our website contact form or when contacting us by email.
  • Client and business correspondence: information contained in emails, proposals, contracts, advertising plans, reports, presentations, meeting notes and other business communications.
  • Website and technical information: information about your visit to our website, such as your IP address, browser and device information, pages visited, referral sources and interactions with our website, where collected through analytics, cookies or similar technologies.
  • Advertising-related information: campaign performance data and information required to plan, manage, measure and report on advertising campaigns.
  • Client-supplied audience data: customer lists and related identifiers supplied by clients for use in advertising platforms, where necessary to deliver our services.

We may receive personal information directly from you, from your organisation, from clients in connection with our services, or automatically through our website and associated technologies.

Please do not submit sensitive personal information through our website unless it is specifically requested and there is an appropriate reason to provide it.

03How we use personal information

We use personal information where necessary to:

  • Respond to enquiries and requests for information.
  • Prepare proposals and quotations and manage prospective client relationships.
  • Provide and administer our advertising and marketing services.
  • Manage client accounts, contracts, billing and business communications.
  • Set up, manage, measure and report on advertising campaigns on behalf of clients.
  • Operate, maintain, secure and improve our website.
  • Understand website usage and measure the effectiveness of our website activity.
  • Comply with legal obligations and establish, exercise or defend legal claims where necessary.

We do not use personal information to send direct marketing emails promoting our own services. We may publish organic content on our social media channels as part of our general business activities.

04Our lawful bases for processing

We process personal information only where we have a lawful basis under applicable data protection legislation. Depending on the circumstances, these bases may include:

  • Legitimate interests: to respond to business enquiries, manage client relationships, administer our business, maintain website security and improve our services, where these interests are not overridden by your rights and interests.
  • Contract: where processing is necessary to enter into or perform a contract with you, or to take steps you request before entering into a contract.
  • Legal obligation: where we need to process information to comply with applicable legal or regulatory requirements.
  • Consent: where required by law, including for non-essential cookies and similar website tracking technologies.

Where we rely on consent, you may withdraw it at any time. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before consent was withdrawn.

The lawful basis we rely on depends on the particular information and purpose for which it is processed.

05Cookies, analytics and advertising technologies

Our website uses Google Analytics 4 (GA4) to help us understand how visitors use our website and assess website performance.

We may also use cookies, pixels, tags and similar technologies associated with services such as Google, Meta and LinkedIn, where these are implemented on our website. Depending on the services used, these technologies may help us measure advertising effectiveness, understand website traffic, attribute visits to campaigns or support advertising-related audience measurement.

These technologies may collect information such as your IP address, device and browser information, website activity and interactions with our website. Relevant service providers may process this information in accordance with their own privacy policies.

We will obtain consent before using non-essential cookies or similar technologies where required by law. Where applicable, you will be able to manage your cookie preferences through our website's cookie consent mechanism.

Information about the cookies and similar technologies used on our website, their purposes and how to manage them will be made available through our website's cookie information and consent settings.

06Personal information processed on behalf of clients

As an advertising agency, we may process personal information on behalf of clients when delivering advertising services. This may include customer lists, email addresses, telephone numbers or other identifiers supplied for use in advertising platforms.

Where clients provide customer lists for advertising purposes, we upload the relevant information directly to the applicable advertising platform. The platform may hash or otherwise transform identifiers as part of its audience-matching processes. We delete the original files and emails containing those attachments once the upload has been completed.

We do not intend to retain these customer-list files as part of our ongoing business records.

Where we process personal information on a client's documented instructions, the client will generally act as the data controller and Rathbone Advertising will act as a data processor, subject to the circumstances and the roles actually performed by each party. In those circumstances, the client is responsible for ensuring that it has an appropriate legal basis for collecting and sharing the information and that individuals receive the required privacy information.

We process such information in accordance with our contractual obligations and applicable data protection law. Where we determine the purposes and means of processing for our own business activities, we may act as a data controller instead.

07How we store and share personal information

We use cloud-based and business software services to store, create, manage and share business files, including client advertising plans, reports, presentations and related documentation. These services include Microsoft OneDrive, Google Docs, Google Sheets, Google Slides and Adobe software used to create and manage PDF documents.

Personal information may also be processed or stored through the systems used to operate our website, receive and manage emails, and provide advertising services.

Where necessary, we may share personal information with:

  • Advertising and analytics providers, including Google, Meta and LinkedIn, where their services are used for website measurement, campaign management or related purposes.
  • Technology and service providers, such as website hosting, email, cloud storage and business software providers.
  • Clients and relevant business contacts, where necessary to deliver our services or respond to a request.
  • Professional advisers, including accountants, lawyers and insurers, where appropriate.
  • Regulators, public authorities or other third parties, where required by law or necessary to protect our legal rights.

We share information only where appropriate for the relevant purpose and subject to applicable legal and contractual requirements.

08International transfers

Some of our service providers may process personal information outside the United Kingdom.

Where personal information is transferred internationally, we will take the steps required by applicable data protection law to ensure that the transfer is lawful and that appropriate safeguards are in place where necessary. These may include an applicable UK adequacy regulation or decision, or approved contractual safeguards.

Where required, further information about relevant international transfer safeguards can be requested by contacting us using the details in section 1.

09How long we retain personal information

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, taking account of the nature of the information, our relationship with the relevant individual or organisation, applicable legal and accounting requirements, and any need to establish, exercise or defend legal claims.

Retention periods may therefore vary depending on the type of information and the circumstances in which it is processed. When information is no longer required, we will take appropriate steps to delete it or anonymise it, subject to any legal requirement to retain it.

Where customer-list files are supplied by clients for advertising uploads, we delete the original files and emails containing those attachments once the upload has been completed, as described in section 6.

10How we protect personal information

We take appropriate technical and organisational measures designed to protect personal information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse, alteration or disclosure.

Access to personal information is limited to those who need it for legitimate business purposes. However, no method of storing or transmitting information can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

11Your data protection rights

Depending on the circumstances and subject to applicable legal requirements, you may have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal information we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Request erasure of your personal information.
  • Request restriction of our processing.
  • Object to certain types of processing, including processing based on legitimate interests.
  • Request the transfer of certain personal information to you or another organisation, where the right to data portability applies.
  • Withdraw consent where we rely on consent to process your information.

Your right to object: You may have the right to object to certain processing of your personal information, particularly where we rely on legitimate interests. If you object, we will consider your request in accordance with applicable data protection law.

To exercise your rights, please contact us at contact@rathboneadvertising.com. We may need to verify your identity before responding.

You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) if you are concerned about how we use your personal information.

The ICO's website is https://ico.org.uk/.

12Third-party websites

Our website may contain links to websites operated by other organisations. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those websites. We recommend reviewing their privacy policies before providing personal information.

13Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our business, website, services or legal obligations. The latest version will be published on our website with the date of the most recent update.
